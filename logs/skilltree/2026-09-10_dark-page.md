## Drawer no longer covers the tree

Peter: right-most hexes disappeared under the panel when selected. `fitAroundPanel()` in
`skilltree.js` pads `.skilltree-wrap` on the right by exactly (wrap right edge − panel left edge +
16 px) whenever the panel opens (articles and the origin sheet), so the SVG rescales into the space
that is left; padding clears on close and recomputes on resize. No-op at ≤720 px where the panel is a
bottom sheet. Padding transitions at the drawer's 260 ms easing (`theme.scss`, reduced-motion off).
Chosen over translating the SVG because `.skilltree` clips overflow and a shift would have cut the
Biosocial/Criminology labels.

## Pinned node lifted above welds and labels; slower make-room

Peter: connections stayed on top of a selected hex. Cause: neighbour welds (`gBridge`) are drawn
above the node layer by design, and labels above that; at 1.8x the enlarged tile ran under them. Fix:
a `gTop` layer appended last in the SVG; the pinned node is moved into it on click and returned to
`gN` by `unpin()` (module-level `nodesLayer`). Long curved edges were already beneath the nodes.
The container padding transition doubled from 260 to 520 ms per Peter.

## Lineage in the panel

Peter: connected articles should reference each other in the detail panel. New "Lineage" section
(`kinHTML()` in `skilltree.js`, between the area dots and "What I did") lists "Builds on" (edges into
the node) and "Built on by" (edges out of it) as row-buttons carrying the connected article's year,
title and colour rule; clicking one runs that node's toggle (same as clicking its hex) and scrolls it
into view. `EDGES`, `NODE_BY_ID` and `nodeToggles` are now module-level, filled in `draw()`. Make-room
transition is 1000 ms with a symmetric ease after two rounds of "slower".
