**Status:** DRAFT, not yet approved. Peter logged out before reviewing (2026-10-21); review at the start of the next session.


## Context

Peter's idea (2026-10-21): the year labels on each ring are inert text. He wants them to work:
hovering a year highlights every paper from that year; clicking it opens a view of just those
papers, staggered in a hex grid with titles only; picking one from that view pulls up that single
hex with its normal detail card. Today the only way to compare a year's papers is to scan the
ring. The outcome is a second way into the tree, by time, that lands on the same card every other
path lands on.

**Assumption, flagged:** "pull up just that hex" is read as: the year view closes, the tree pins
and flips that hex and opens the normal right-hand card, exactly as the panel's lineage rows do
today. No new card type. If Peter meant the year view should stay open with one hex enlarged
inside it, that is a different build; say so.

## How the tree works today (what the plan reuses)

- `www/skilltree.js` (740 lines, one IIFE). `draw(tree)` builds the SVG from `www/tree.json`.
  Nodes are `<g class="node" data-id>` with `<image>` + `hex-shade` + `hex-ring`; `n.year` and
  `n.ring` are on every node. Year labels are `<text class="year-label">` in the top `gL`
  layer (lines 641-644), drawn from `meta.rings[]` (`k`, `year`, `label_x`, `label_y`,
  `label_over_hex`, `path`, `n_used`). Ring guides are `<path class="ring-guide">` (line 437)
  drawn from the same array, so a `data-year` can go on both.
- Hover rule (CLAUDE.md): nothing geometric on hover; highlight = ring stroke up, shade eased.
  The lineage highlight is the model: `setLineage()` toggles `has-hot` on the svg and `.hot` on
  edges, CSS dims the rest (`svg.has-hot .edge:not(.hot)`, theme.scss 618).
- Click = `nodeToggles[id](e)` (module-level map, line 539): unpins, lifts the `<g>` to `gTop`,
  adds `pinned flipping lit`, opens the panel with `openPanel()`, sets `#id` in the hash.
  `openPanel`'s `.sp-kin` handler (lines 353-368) already drives this from HTML outside the SVG
  with a fake event and `scrollIntoView`. The year view will do the same.
- Overlays are portaled to `<body>` (lines 18-20) because Quarto's content column caps z-index;
  the panel sits at `z-index: 1100`. The tooltip `tip` is shared (`placeTipAt`, `hideTip`).
- `document` click handler (line 666) unpins unless the target is inside `.node`, `.origin-node`,
  `.rank-node` or `.skilltree-panel`. Esc unpins (665). `openFromHash()` (670-690) handles
  `#me`, `#rank-<year>`, `#<id>`.
- Styles: `.skilltree*` block at the end of `theme.scss` (549 on); `--st-*` tokens on
  `.skilltree-wrap`; the panel carries its own `--sp-*` copies because it lives outside the wrap.
  `.year-label` at 609-612 (26px mono, halo stroke; `.over-hex` inverts). Reduced-motion
  overrides at 675-681. Phone breakpoint is 720px (813, 870).
- `skilltree.qmd` holds the scaffold divs and the `?v=` cache-buster (line 26, currently
  `2026-10-04l`); bump on every JS change (CLAUDE.md build gotcha).

## Changes

### 1. `www/skilltree.js`

**a. Year labels become controls (lines 641-644).** For each `rg` in `m.rings`:
- Wrap in a `<g class="year-node" data-year tabindex="0" role="button">` holding a transparent
  hit `<rect>` (about 2.2 x 1.6 times the text box, same idea as the rank bead's hit circle) and
  the existing `<text class="year-label">`. `aria-label`: `"2023: 4 articles. Show them."`
  (count from `rg.n_used`; singular handled).
- Put `data-year` on each `.ring-guide` too (line 437).
- Hover / focus: `setYearHot(year, true)`; leave / blur: `setYearHot(year, false)` (but if the
  year view is open, keep it). Show the shared tooltip: title = the year, meta = `"4 articles ·
  click to browse"` (deadpan, Peter's voice; no new copy beyond that).
- Click / Enter / Space: `openYearView(year)`.

**b. `setYearHot(year, on)`** next to `setLineage` (line 504): toggles `has-year` on the svg,
`.year-hot` on every `.node` with `n.year === year`, on that year's `.ring-guide` and on the
`.year-node`. Keep a `nodesByYear` map built once in `draw()`. If a node is pinned, leave it
alone (the `.lit` rules outrank). No transforms, no filters on `<image>`.

**c. Year view overlay.** New scaffold in `skilltree.qmd` (next to the panel):
`<div class="skilltree-yearview" id="skilltree-yearview" role="dialog" aria-modal="true"
hidden>` with a header (`.yv-year`, `.yv-count`), a close button `.yv-close`, and a `.yv-grid`
container. Portal it to `<body>` with the panel (line 20).

`openYearView(year)`:
- `unpin()` first (closes any card), hide the tooltip, then fill `.yv-grid` with one
  `<button class="yv-tile" data-id>` per node of that year, ordered as on the ring (by `angle`,
  then `cv_number`). A tile is a small inline `<svg>` (reuse `hexPath`, the sticker
  `<image href=n.sticker_src>`, a `hex-ring` stroked in `n.colour` at the node's role weight) plus
  the title in a `<span class="yv-title">`. Titles only, per Peter: no venue, no role, no pips.
- Stagger: honeycomb by JS. Tile width `tw` (desktop 150px, phone 110px), row pitch
  `0.78 * tile height`; `cols = max(1, min(N, floor(gridWidth / tw)))`; tile `i` sits at
  `col = i % cols`, `row = floor(i / cols)`, `left = col * tw + (row odd ? tw / 2 : 0)`,
  `top = row * pitch`. Absolute positioning inside `.yv-grid` (position: relative; height set to
  the last row's bottom). Recompute on `resize` while open. Odd rows shift right; if the last
  row is short it stays left-aligned, which reads as the ring's own raggedness.
- Tile click: `e.stopPropagation()`, `closeYearView()`, then `nodeToggles[id](fake)` and
  `scrollIntoView({block: 'center', behavior})` on the hex, exactly as `.sp-kin` does (lines
  355-367), with the same reduced-motion check. The card opens with its own `#id` hash.
- Backdrop click and `.yv-close` close it; Esc closes it (extend the handler at line 665: year
  view first, then `unpin()`). Focus moves to the first tile on open and back to the year label
  on close.
- Hash: `openYearView` sets `#year-2023` via `replaceState`; `closeYearView` clears it unless a
  node toggle is about to set its own. `openFromHash()` gains a `year-(\d{4})` branch before the
  node branch.
- Add `.skilltree-yearview` and `.year-node` to the exclusion list in the document click
  handler (line 666) so opening the view or clicking inside it does not fire `unpin()` a second
  time after the tile has pinned a node.

**d. Legend (line 650 block):** one more `item()`: a small year glyph (`20xx` in the mono face)
with the text "Click a year to browse it". Cheap discoverability; the labels otherwise look inert.
