# 2026-10-21 — Year view: year labels hover-highlight and click-to-browse

## What happened
Peter approved the year-view plan (`plans/2026-10-21_year-view.md`) as written; it was built on the laptop the same
evening. The plan had been written and pushed from the other machine, so this clone was fetched first (2 commits behind).

Changed: `www/skilltree.js` (year labels become `<g class="year-node">` controls; `setYearHot`; year view open / close /
layout / pick; `#year-<yyyy>` deep link; Esc and the document click handler know about the view; legend line),
`theme.scss` (year-hot dim and highlight, `.skilltree-yearview` block, `.lg-year`), `skilltree.qmd` (scaffold,
`?v=2026-10-21a`), `CLAUDE.md` (Interaction bullet).

## Deviations from the plan
- **Hit box.** The plan said ~2.2 x 1.6 times the text box. Every label sits in an empty cell with the neighbouring
  hexes half a hex (50 units) either side, and the rank beads (2019, 2020, 2024) 43 units to the right; labels draw
  above both, so a +/-68 box would have stolen their hover and click. Built at +/-46 (inside the cell) x 42, trimmed
  2 units short of a rank bead on the same ring.
- **Rows do not interlock.** The plan's 0.78 x tile-height row pitch would push the next row's hex tip into the
  three-line titles above (a title runs to within ~4 px of the next row's hex centre line). Rows are offset half a tile
  horizontally, as planned, but stacked with a 12 px gap.
- Backdrop 0.96 rather than 0.92: at 0.92 the page title read through the overlay.

## Verification (Playwright, Chromium, 1280x900 and 390x844, against a local server on `docs/`)
Hover 2025: 6 nodes `.year-hot` at opacity 1, every other node 0.3 once the 160 ms fade ends, tooltip "6 articles ·
click to browse"; leave clears all. Click: overlay visible, 6 tiles, `#year-2025`. Second tile: overlay closes, that
node `.pinned.lit`, panel open with the same title, hash `#<id>`; Esc closes the panel. `#year-2026` on load: 9 tiles,
staggered (6 + 3 at +75 px on desktop; 2 per row on the phone). Keyboard: focus 2023, Enter opens with focus on the first
tile, Esc closes with focus back on the label. Every node and rank-bead centre is still topmost at its own point;
nothing just outside any hit box hits a year node. No page errors.
