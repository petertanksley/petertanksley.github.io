# 2026-10-01 — Fun-feature idea list

Brainstorm with Bob. Peter: "This website is honestly a lot more fun than it is professional. I like that."
Nothing built; these are candidates, unranked except where noted. Guardrails from `CLAUDE.md` apply to all of
them: never joke about responders dying, never name datasets, real people and journals stay anonymous.

- **404 dungeon floor** (Bob's first pick): a missing URL means "you fell through the trapdoor"; pixel-art
  sub-basement in the trapdoor scene's style, one way back up. Cheap, reuses existing art direction.
- **Rare spawns**: one of Peter's dogs turns up in a random hex on roughly 1 page load in 50; spotting it can be
  a visitor achievement. Mostly JS plus one or two stickers from the existing `4_stickers/` pipeline.
- **Seasonal scenes**: a Halloween variant of the hearth or trapdoor scene for October, swapped on a date check.
  One wide-scene generation.
- **Reviewer 2 bestiary**: an illustrated catalog of reviewer archetypes (The Scope Creeper, The Cite-My-Work
  Wraith, The Desk-Reject Speedrunner). Archetypes only, no identifiable reviewers or journals. Natural LHT #2.
- **Achievements for unseen work**: reviews completed (from the `/bob review` archive), R&R survived, IRB
  amendments. Expanded in the achievements discussion the same day.
- **Inventory panel** on the character sheet: R as main weapon, Stata as cursed heirloom, the Research Scientist
  lab coat as armor, coffee as a consumable.
- **Nameless advice skull**: click for a line from a list of snark. Stays nameless per the sticker rules.
